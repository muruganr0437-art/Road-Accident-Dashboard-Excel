# 🚗 Road Accident Analysis Dashboard (Excel Project)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end data analysis and dashboard solution built using Microsoft Excel to analyze road accident data and uncover key insights related to casualties, road conditions, vehicle involvement, and environmental factors.

The dashboard transforms raw accident records into interactive visualizations, helping stakeholders identify accident patterns and support data-driven road safety decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Analyze road accident data to:

* Track overall casualty statistics
* Understand accident severity distribution
* Identify monthly accident trends
* Evaluate the impact of road types and road surfaces
* Compare urban and rural accident occurrences
* Analyze accidents under different lighting conditions
* Support road safety and transportation planning

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Road Type Impact

Single carriageways accounted for the highest number of casualties, highlighting a critical area for road safety improvements.

### Road Surface Conditions

Most accidents occurred on dry road surfaces, indicating that traffic volume plays a significant role in accident frequency.

### Severity Distribution

Slight casualties represented more than 84% of all recorded casualties, while fatal casualties accounted for less than 2%.

### Vehicle Involvement

Cars were involved in the majority of recorded casualties compared to other vehicle categories.

### Location Analysis

Urban areas experienced a higher number of casualties than rural areas due to increased traffic density.

### Light Condition Analysis

Most casualties occurred during daylight conditions, suggesting traffic exposure is a major contributing factor.
